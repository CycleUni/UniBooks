UniBooks

UniBooks is a second-hand textbook search and matching platform for university students in Taiwan. Students find the textbooks they need by ISBN, title or course, list books they no longer use, and arrange on-campus meetups with classmates through in-app chat. The platform only matches people and never handles payments.

Browsing books needs no account. Listing, ordering and chat require signing in with a school email or a Google account. With Sign in with Google, UniBooks receives only your email address, name and profile picture, used solely to create and identify your account.

UniBooks 是專為台灣大專院校學生打造的二手教科書搜尋與媒合平台：用 ISBN、書名或課程找書、刊登不再使用的書，並透過站內訊息與同學約定校園面交。平台不經手任何款項。
